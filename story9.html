Echoes in the Library

Author: Tobias Wren

Genre: Mystery

Synopsis: Whispers in a closed library lead a night guard to a century-old secret.

Chapter 1: After Hours

Samir locked the main doors at ten and began his rounds. On the third floor, a book fell from a shelf with no one near it. Then another. Then a whisper: 'Read me.'

The fallen book was a diary dated 1921. Its last page ended mid-sentence, as if the writer had been interrupted. Samir turned the page over and found a hand-drawn map.
