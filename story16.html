Treasure of the Jade Reef

Author: Marco Delgado

Genre: Adventure

Synopsis: Two siblings follow their late father's map to a sunken pirate ship.

Chapter 1: The Map in the Tackle Box

Lia found the map folded inside Dad's old tackle box, drawn in his messy handwriting. At the bottom he had written one line: 'For my brave ones. Don't tell your mother.'

Her brother Kiko grinned and tightened his life vest. By sunrise their little boat was bobbing toward a reef the color of jade, and beneath it, something golden glinted.
