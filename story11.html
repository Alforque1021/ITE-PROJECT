The Last Garden on Mars

Author: Noor Haddad

Genre: Sci-Fi

Synopsis: A botanist protects the final seeds of Earth as the colony runs out of air.

Chapter 1: Green Dome

Dr. Amani pressed her palm to the dome glass. Beyond it the red dust howled. Inside, one tomato plant had just flowered, the first in two hundred days.

Oxygen reserves read nine percent. The council wanted to shut the garden down and ration the air. Amani looked at the flower and made a decision they would not like.
