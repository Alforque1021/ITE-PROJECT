Do Not Open Room 13

Author: Lydia Crane

Genre: Horror

Synopsis: A hotel night clerk is told never to open Room 13, and the bell rings from inside.

Chapter 1: Night Shift

'Whatever you hear,' said the old manager, handing over the keys, 'do not open Room 13.' Dan nodded and thought it was a joke. At 2 a.m. the service bell rang.

It rang again, slow and patient. The room's light blinked on under the door. Then a polite voice called through the wood: 'Room service, please. I have been waiting very long.'

End of chapter
