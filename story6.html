Seventeen Summers

Author: Hana Rivera

Genre: Romance

Synopsis: A girl returns to her grandmother's island and to the boy she never forgot.

Chapter 1: Salt Wind

The ferry docked just as the sun burned orange over the water. Ivy gripped her suitcase and scanned the pier, telling herself she was not looking for anyone.

'You still pack too much,' said a voice behind her. She turned. Theo had grown taller, and his smile was exactly the same as the one she had carried for seventeen summers.
