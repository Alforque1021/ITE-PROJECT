Coffee at Platform Nine

Author: Mika Tan

Genre: Romance

Synopsis: Two strangers keep missing the same train, and meeting at the same cafe.

Chapter 1: Oat Milk, No Sugar

Nina ordered the same drink every morning at 7:42. Today the barista had already written her name on the cup, and beneath it, in different handwriting: 'Your train is late. Sit with me?'

She looked up. A tall stranger with ink-stained fingers pulled out the chair beside him. Outside, the train announcement echoed, and for once Nina was glad to wait.
