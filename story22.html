The Violin in Room 4B

Author: Anya Petrova

Genre: Drama

Synopsis: A retired musician's neighbor hears her playing and slowly rebuilds her will to perform.

Chapter 1: Thin Walls

Every evening at six, the same sad melody drifted through the wall. Leo, a tired nurse, had never met the woman in 4B, but he knew the song by heart.

One night the music stopped mid-note. He knocked on her door with a bowl of soup. 'You stopped,' he said softly. She looked at him, eyes wet. 'I forgot how it ends.'
