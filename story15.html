The Scarecrow Remembers

Author: Elias Mourne

Genre: Horror

Synopsis: On harvest night, a scarecrow climbs down from its pole to find its killer.

Chapter 1: Harvest Moon

The crows were the first to notice. They scattered at dusk, screaming, as the old scarecrow twitched on its pole. Straw rustled. Buttons for eyes turned toward the farmhouse.

'Thirty years,' the thing rasped, dropping into the corn. 'Thirty years I watched. Tonight I remember who tied me here.' A light flicked on in the farmhouse window.

End of chapter
