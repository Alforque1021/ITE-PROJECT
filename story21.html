Grandpa's Haunted Wi-Fi

Author: Lola Park

Genre: Comedy

Synopsis: Grandpa insists the router is haunted, and the family's phones start agreeing.

Chapter 1: Spooky Signal

'It's a ghost,' said Grandpa, pointing at the blinking router. Mia rolled her eyes. Then her phone buzzed with a new network name: 'BOO_FREE_WIFI.'

The password hint read: 'my favorite food.' Grandpa whispered, 'Tapsilog.' The connection snapped on at full speed, and somewhere upstairs, a very satisfied ghost burped.

End of chapter
