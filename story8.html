Case of the Missing Violin

Author: Priya Nair

Genre: Mystery

Synopsis: A famous violin vanishes from a locked music room during a school concert.

Chapter 1: Locked Door

Jun was tuning his cello when the scream echoed down the hall. The Stradivarius case sat open on the stage, empty, velvet still warm from where it had rested.

Every door had been locked from inside. Jun noticed a faint smell of lavender and a single rosin-dusted fingerprint on the window. 'Nobody leaves,' he said. 'The thief is still here.'

End of chapter
