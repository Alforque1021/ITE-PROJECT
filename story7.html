The Silent Clocktower

Author: Victor Hale

Genre: Mystery

Synopsis: The town clock stops at 3:17 every night, and so does the heart of a different resident.

Chapter 1: 3:17

Detective Mara Lind arrived in Ashgrove on a rainy Tuesday. The clocktower loomed over the square, its hands frozen. 'It stopped when old Mr. Pell died,' said the innkeeper. 'And again last night.'

Mara climbed the narrow stairs alone. Dust lay thick everywhere except one patch near the gears, where fresh footprints led to a hidden door. It was slightly open.

End of chapter
