Shadow Protocol

Author: Cassian Rook

Genre: Thriller

Synopsis: A cyber agent discovers her own name on a list of traitors she never met.

Chapter 1: Access Denied

Agent Vera typed her password and the screen blinked red. ACCESS DENIED. She tried again. Then a new window opened, listing names. Hers was highlighted at the top.

She pulled the plug and slipped the drive into her sleeve. 'Someone wants me burned,' she murmured. Down the hall, footsteps stopped outside the door.
