What the River Carried

Author: Samuel Okoye

Genre: Drama

Synopsis: After a flood, three siblings find the things that tie them to each other.

Chapter 1: High Water

The river took the porch, the garden, and Papa's old boots. When the water finally fell, the three siblings stood in the mud, no one speaking.

Ruth was the first to kneel. She pulled a muddy photograph from the silt and wiped it with her sleeve. Their mother's face smiled up at them. 'We start here,' she whispered.
