Project Aurora Seven

Author: Kai Mendel

Genre: Sci-Fi

Synopsis: A crew wakes from cryosleep to find their ship has traveled forty years too far.

Chapter 1: Wake Protocol

Commander Ilsa opened her eyes to flashing red lights. 'Status,' she croaked. The ship's AI paused. 'Commander, we are not where we are supposed to be. We are not when we are supposed to be, either.'

Outside the viewport hung a star she did not recognize, ringed by a thousand glittering machines. Someone, or something, had been busy while they slept.
