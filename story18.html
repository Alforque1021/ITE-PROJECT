Summit of the Silver Peak

Author: Henrik Larsen

Genre: Adventure

Synopsis: A mountain guide leads three strangers up a peak that has never been climbed.

Chapter 1: Base Camp

'Nobody has reached the top,' said Anika, tightening her pack. 'Not because it is impossible, but because it does not want visitors.' The three clients laughed nervously.

At dawn the clouds parted and the Silver Peak shone like a blade. Anika took the first step on the ice. Behind her, the others followed, their breath rising like prayers.
