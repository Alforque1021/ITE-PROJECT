Zero Hour in Manila

Author: Rico Santos

Genre: Thriller

Synopsis: A bomb squad rookie has sixty minutes to stop a plot that hides in plain sight.

Chapter 1: 60:00

The timer on the message read 60:00. Officer Ana Reyes stared at the screen as traffic roared along Roxas Boulevard. 'It's a prank,' someone said. The clock began counting.

She grabbed her radio. 'Every unit, check the bayside lamp posts.' Her voice was steady, though her hands shook. Somewhere in the crowd, a man in a yellow cap watched her go.
