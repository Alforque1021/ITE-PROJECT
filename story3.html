The Moonwell Witch

Author: SabineOrrel

Genre: Fantasy

Synopsis: Every full moon, a village girl trades secrets with a witch at the bottom of a well.

Chapter 1: One Silver Coin

Tala lowered the bucket three times before the water answered. A voice rose from below, soft as wet silk. 'You are early, little one. Did you bring the coin?'

She dropped it into the dark. The water glowed, and an old woman's face formed in the ripples. 'Ask,' said the witch. Tala swallowed. 'Where did my brother go?'

End of chapter
