Samurai of the Falling Petals

Author: Kenji Aoki

Genre: Historical

Synopsis: A masterless samurai protects a village during the last cherry blossom spring.

Chapter 1: The Wandering Blade

Petals drifted over the empty road as Ryo, a ronin without a lord, walked into the village of Sakurazaka. A child offered him tea. He bowed, surprised by the kindness.

That night the bandits came. Ryo set down his cup, rose slowly, and drew his sword. 'This village is under my protection,' he said. Petals fell between them like snow.
