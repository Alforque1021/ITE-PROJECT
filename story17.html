Across the Burning Desert

Author: Amira Said

Genre: Adventure

Synopsis: A young courier crosses the Sahar with a sealed scroll and a stubborn camel.

Chapter 1: Water and Scroll

Zayd counted his water skins twice. Three. The desert would need five. His camel, Biscuit, groaned as if she understood the math. Behind them, the city lights vanished.

On the second night a sandstorm swallowed the stars. Zayd pressed the scroll to his chest and whispered to Biscuit, 'If we live, I will buy you all the dates in the world.'
