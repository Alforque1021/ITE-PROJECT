Mother's Recipe Book

Author: Carmen Dela Cruz

Genre: Drama

Synopsis: A daughter cooks through her late mother's notes and rediscovers their family.

Chapter 1: Page One

The book smelled like garlic and old paper. On the first page, Mama had written: 'Cooking is how I say what I cannot say.' Joy sat on the kitchen floor and cried.

She tied on the apron and boiled the rice. The scent filled the house. For the first time since the funeral, it felt like someone was humming in the next room.
