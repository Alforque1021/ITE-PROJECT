Crown of Thorn and Starlight

Author: Rowan Ashby

Genre: Fantasy

Synopsis: A disgraced knight swears to guard a princess who can speak to stars.

Chapter 1: The Oath

Ser Aldric knelt in the cold chapel while the bells rang for a kingdom that no longer wanted him. The princess watched from the balcony, whispering to the sky as if it whispered back.

'They say you killed your captain,' she said at last. Aldric kept his eyes down. 'Then they have told you only half the story, Your Highness.' Above them a single star flickered, as if in agreement.
