The Dragon's Last Egg

Author: Elara Voss

Genre: Fantasy

Synopsis: A young shepherd finds the final dragon egg and must hide it from a hungry kingdom.

Chapter 1: The Warm Stone

Mira found the stone in a snowdrift behind the sheep pen. It was warm, and it hummed like a kettle. She tucked it inside her coat and walked home beneath a sky full of ash-colored clouds.

By midnight the stone cracked. A tiny green eye opened, blinked at her, and sneezed a spark onto the blanket. Mira smiled. Somewhere far away, the king's hunters had just begun to search.
