The Scribe of Alexandria

Author: Theron Marsh

Genre: Historical

Synopsis: A young scribe races to save scrolls before the great library burns.

Chapter 1: Smoke on the Harbor

Smoke rose over the harbor of Alexandria. Nikos dropped his reed pen and ran toward the library, his sandals slapping marble. Soldiers shouted. Somewhere a scroll cracked in the heat.

He grabbed as many papyrus rolls as his arms could hold and fled into the narrow alley. Behind him, a thousand years of thought began to glow orange.
