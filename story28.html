The Man Who Knew Too Little

Author: Dmitri Vance

Genre: Thriller

Synopsis: An ordinary cashier is chased across the city for a phone call he never made.

Chapter 1: Wrong Number

Lucas was stocking milk when his phone rang. The voice on the line said only, 'It is done. Delete everything.' Then it hung up. Lucas had no idea what to delete.

By evening, two men in gray suits were waiting outside his apartment. He ran down the fire escape, heart pounding, still holding a carton of milk he had forgotten to pay for.
