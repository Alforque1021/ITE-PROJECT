The Great Pancake Disaster

Author: Penny Walsh

Genre: Comedy

Synopsis: A school bake-off goes wrong when the pancake batter starts growing.

Chapter 1: Too Much Yeast

Oscar was certain that more yeast meant more fluffy. By the time the judges arrived, his batter had climbed out of the bowl, down the table, and was gently pushing a teacher toward the door.

'Is it supposed to burp?' asked Principal Dunn. Oscar smiled weakly. 'It's a, um, rising star.' The batter burped again, and the whole gym smelled like warm bread.

End of chapter
