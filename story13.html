The House at Dead Hollow

Author: Morgan Blackwood

Genre: Horror

Synopsis: A family inherits a house where the walls whisper their names at night.

Chapter 1: Welcome Home

The key turned too easily, as though the lock had been waiting. Dad laughed, Mom said it smelled like old roses, and little Ben said nothing. He was staring at the stairs.

That night, a whisper slid through the vents: 'Benjamin.' He pulled the blanket over his head. Then, from the closet, a second voice answered, in his own tone: 'I'm here.'

End of chapter
