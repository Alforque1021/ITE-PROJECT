My Cat Runs for Mayor

Author: Felix Bright

Genre: Comedy

Synopsis: When the town's mayor resigns, the only candidate on the ballot is a very fat cat.

Chapter 1: Vote Whiskers

It started as a joke. Sam wrote 'Mr. Whiskers' on the ballot form to annoy his sister. Then forty neighbors did the same. By Friday the cat had a campaign poster.

'My platform is simple,' Sam read aloud at the rally, 'more naps, more tuna, fewer Mondays.' The crowd roared. Mr. Whiskers yawned and fell asleep on the podium.

End of chapter
