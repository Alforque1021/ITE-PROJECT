Robot Named Pebble

Author: Jonah Frost

Genre: Sci-Fi

Synopsis: A junkyard robot learns what friendship means from a lost little girl.

Chapter 1: Rusty Hello

Pebble had been switched off for ten years when the girl crawled into the junkyard, crying. A loose wire sparked, and his one good eye flickered awake. 'Hello,' he said. 'You are leaking.'

'That's tears,' she sniffled. Pebble searched his database. 'Tears. Cause: sadness. Solution: ...unknown.' He held out a rusty hand anyway. She took it.

End of chapter
