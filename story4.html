Letters to a Lighthouse

Author: Daniela Quinn

Genre: Romance

Synopsis: A lonely keeper begins answering letters that wash ashore in glass bottles.

Chapter 1: The First Bottle

Elias had kept the light for eleven years without a single visitor. So when the bottle rolled onto the rocks that morning, he held it longer than he should have.

The note was short: 'To whoever is out there, I am afraid of the sea and I live beside it. Does it get easier?' Elias smiled for the first time in months, and reached for a pen.
