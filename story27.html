Letters from the Front

Author: Eleanor Whitby

Genre: Historical

Synopsis: A nurse and a soldier exchange letters during the long winter of 1917.

Chapter 1: The First Letter

Dear Miss Harlow, the letter began. I have no one to write to, so I picked your name from the hospital roster. Forgive me. Nurse Edith read it twice by candlelight.

She wrote back that same night: 'You are forgiven, Private Cole. Tell me about home.' Outside, the guns thundered, but inside the little tent, a quiet friendship began.

End of chapter
